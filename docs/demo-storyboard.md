# Stillframe demo storyboard

**Long pages. One screenshot.**

Use a sample trail guide with content below the fold. Before recording, grant Stillframe screen recording and Accessibility permissions. Open a blank document for the paste demonstration.

| Time | On-screen action | Cursor or click | Caption | Transition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | The trail guide continues below the visible window. | Scroll briefly to reveal the next section, then return to the top. | The page keeps going. | Cut to Stillframe |
| 4–9s | Stillframe opens the region picker. | Click **Scroll** and drag around the guide content. | Select what you want to capture. | Match cut on the selection |
| 9–13s | The capture panel shows the first frame. | Move to **Scroll & Capture**. | Stillframe grabs the first frame. | Gentle push in |
| 13–20s | The page moves down and the frame count grows to three. | Click **Scroll & Capture**, then repeat. | Scroll and stitch the next sections. | Cut between captures |
| 20–26s | The complete guide appears as one tall image. | Click **Finish**, then paste into the blank document. | One screenshot, ready to paste. | Match cut to the pasted image |
| 26–30s | Show the hero with the GitHub repository name. | Move the cursor beside the **Getting started** link. | Build Stillframe from GitHub. | Fade out |
